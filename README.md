# 7-Day South Indian Diabetic Vegetarian Meal Plan

## Project Overview

This project contains a 7-day South Indian vegetarian meal plan designed for a 60-year-old person with diabetes and a less active lifestyle.

The plan focuses on balanced nutrition, locally available ingredients, portion control, and variety in everyday meals.

## Person Profile

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Age | 60 years |
| Lifestyle | Less Active |
| Health Condition | Diabetes |
| Diet Preference | Vegetarian |
| Cuisine | South Indian |
| Allergies | Nuts and Brinjal |
| Dislikes | Millets |

## Meal Plan Structure

The meal plan covers 7 days, starting from Monday.

Each day includes four meals:

1. Breakfast
2. Lunch
3. Evening Snack
4. Dinner

**Total planned meals:** 28

## Dietary Guidelines

- Use locally available South Indian ingredients.
- Include vegetables, pulses, and whole grains.
- Prefer whole grains such as brown rice, hand-pounded rice, and whole wheat.
- Maintain controlled carbohydrate portions.
- Include fibre-rich vegetables and protein sources.
- Avoid excessive oil, sugar, and salt.
- Avoid nuts and brinjal due to allergies.
- Avoid all millet-based dishes.
- Avoid dairy products.
- Ensure different dishes are served throughout the week.
- Avoid extreme dietary restrictions.

## Food Categories Included

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | Idli, pesarattu, adai, oats upma, dalia upma |
| Lunch | Brown rice, hand-pounded rice, kootu, sambar, poriyal |
| Snacks | Guava, papaya, roasted gram, sundal |
| Dinner | Chapati, wheat dosa, vegetable uthappam, phulka |
| Protein Sources | Green gram, chickpeas, moong dal, soya chunks |
| Vegetables | Spinach, beans, cabbage, carrot, pumpkin, snake gourd |

## Important Health Considerations

- Monitor blood glucose levels regularly.
- Adjust carbohydrate portions according to individual glucose readings and medical advice.
- Avoid fruit juices; prefer whole fruits in controlled portions.
- Choose unsweetened beverages.
- Consult a registered dietitian or treating doctor before making significant dietary changes, especially if diabetes medications or insulin are being used.
- Portion sizes may need adjustment based on weight, kidney function, medication, and activity level.

## Project Objective

To provide a practical, affordable, and sustainable South Indian vegetarian meal plan that supports blood glucose management while maintaining nutritional balance and meal variety.
