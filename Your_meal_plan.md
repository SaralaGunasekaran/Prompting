# Meal Plan Generator Prompt

Act as an experienced dietician.

## Person Profile

- Age: [Enter age]
- Lifestyle: [Active / Moderately Active / Less Active]
- Health Condition: [Enter health condition or None]
- Allergy: [Enter food allergies or None]
- Dislikes: [Enter disliked foods]
- Diet: [Vegetarian / Non-Vegetarian / Vegan]
- Cuisine: [Enter preferred cuisine]

## Requirements

- Use locally available and seasonal ingredients.
- Provide balanced portions suitable for the individual's age and lifestyle.
- Consider the person's health condition while planning meals.
- Avoid extreme dietary restrictions.
- Include unique dishes at every meal.
- Generate a complete 7-day meal plan.
- Include Breakfast, Lunch, Snack, and Dinner.
- Include suitable protein, fibre, vegetables,
  and whole grains.
- Specify realistic serving portions for every dish.
- Prefer simple, affordable, homemade recipes.

## Output Format

- Generate one table only.
- Columns: Day | Meal | Dish | Portion.
- Start day names from Monday.
- Include all seven days.
- Include four meals per day.
- Use real and commonly known dish names.
- Mention practical portion sizes using cups,
  grams, pieces, or other suitable measurements.

## Avoid

- Foods that trigger the specified allergies.
- Foods the individual dislikes.
- Ingredients that are difficult to find locally.
- Foods that conflict with the specified diet.
- Repeated dishes throughout the seven days.
- Excessive sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats.
- Extreme calorie restriction or unnecessary food elimination.

## Final Instructions

- Follow every detail in the person profile.
- Do not introduce ingredients that violate
  the allergy restrictions.
- Ensure meal variety throughout the week.
- Keep the plan practical and easy to follow.
- Consider relevant medical conditions and
  recommend professional dietary review when needed.
- Return only the requested table.
